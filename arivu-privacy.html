Privacy Policy — Arivu: AI Quran

Last updated: October 21, 2026

“Arivu: AI Quran” (the “App”) is an offline-first app for reading the Holy Quran in Arabic with English and Tamil translations, tafsir commentary, prayer times, and on-device AI search. This policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you. There are no accounts, no sign-up, and no data entry of any kind. The App has no ability to identify you as an individual.

2. On-Device Data

All Quran content — the Arabic text, English and Tamil translations, tafsir (commentary), surah metadata, search indexes, and AI data packs — is stored locally on your device inside the App’s private storage. This data:

  • Is used only to display content and power reading, search, and AI features.
  • Never leaves your device.
  • Can be removed at any time by deleting a pack in Settings or uninstalling the App.

3. Location (Prayer Times & Qibla)

To calculate prayer times and show the Qibla direction, you may set your location manually from a city list, or allow the App to use GPS once. Your location is used only on your device for these calculations:

  • It is stored locally in the App’s private storage.
  • It is never transmitted to any server and never shared with anyone.
  • You can change or clear it at any time in Settings.

4. AI Smart Search (On-Device)

The App’s AI search feature uses machine-learning models that run entirely on your device. Your queries and all related processing happen locally; nothing is sent to any server.

5. Voice & Text-to-Speech

If you use the App’s voice features, they rely on speech engines provided by your Android device or operating system. Any audio processing performed by those system components is governed by your device manufacturer’s policies, not this one.

6. Internet Access & Content Downloads

The App works fully offline. It connects to the internet only when you explicitly choose to download a content pack (Quran text, tafsir, or AI data). Those downloads are plain content files from our server; no personal information is sent with them.

7. Analytics, Advertising & Tracking

The App does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking services of any kind. It contains no ad SDKs and no data-collection libraries.

8. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Because the App collects no personal information, it shares no data with any third party.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

10. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
